# Banner

Inline message at the top of a region: info, success, warning or danger, with optional action.

## Props
- `tone`: `info` (default) · `success` · `warning` · `danger`.
- `title` (bold line), `children` (detail), `icon` (override), `action` (a small ghost Button).

## Do / don't
- One banner per region. Say what happened and what to do.
