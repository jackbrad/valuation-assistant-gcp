Property Valuation Architecture

Appraisal-faithful RAG on Google Cloud for a large residential real-estate marketplace with a mortgage arm. Siloed PDFs become cited facts in BigQuery, BQML builds the comps the way an appraiser does, three gates control risk, and reviewer feedback improves the data and the models every cycle.

How to walk it in the room

  1. Land. Siloed documents land in Cloud Storage and show up in BigQuery as object tables.
  2. Parse and clean. Document AI handles structure; Gemini takes the scans, checkboxes and tables that span pages. Dataform resolves every document to one property and maps legacy fields to the UAD 3.6 schema.
  3. G1, data gate. Low-confidence or conflicting facts are held for review instead of entering the golden record.
  4. Model. BigQuery holds the cited facts, embeddings and sales. BQML builds the market index, the comp ranker, the adjustment grid and the AVM, with ML.EXPLAIN_PREDICT on every value.
  5. G2, valuation gate. The agent gathers evidence with tools and does the math deterministically. It returns a range, or routes to an appraiser when the evidence is thin.
  6. G3, decision gate. A human signs off on anything that moves money.
  7. Close the loop. Every “this is wrong” goes through review with a second approver, updates the record, re-values affected properties and feeds the next training cycle. The circuit breaker checks the models against real sales.
